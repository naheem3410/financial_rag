# HaqqAI milestones for the funding form

Three milestones. Budget amounts add up to the full request and match the project budget workbook.

## Milestone 1

**Name:** Customer assistants

**Description:** Build the first customer facing assistant on the HaqqAI verification engine, set up for account opening, KYC guidance, product discovery, support, complaints and financial education, with a confidence score on every answer. Make answers faster by reusing stored vectors, and add a tool so an institution's own documents can be loaded without engineering help. Test it with bank staff, and set up secrets management and separate storage for the audit trail.

**Budget amount (naira):** 20,987,778

**Duration (number of days):** 120

**Milestone asset (pdf):** `milestones/HAQQAI_MILESTONE_1.pdf`

## Milestone 2

**Name:** Staff and compliance assistants

**Description:** Build the staff knowledge assistant, set up for HR, IT, operations, customer service copilot and training, plus assistants for credit decision support, compliance and regulatory intelligence. Add the screens that show each check and its source page. Connect one pilot institution's test environment, then test tenant isolation under load and complete an independent penetration test with a retest. Finish the data protection review and a standard data agreement.

**Budget amount (naira):** 19,781,112

**Duration (number of days):** 120

**Milestone asset (pdf):** `milestones/HAQQAI_MILESTONE_2.pdf`

## Milestone 3

**Name:** Live pilot and escalation

**Description:** Deploy HaqqAI at one institution, train its staff, and support it for three months after go live. Add human escalation so declined or high risk answers go to a reviewer, and assistants for AML and KYC, risk and fraud investigation support. Track usage and how many answers pass verification, and work towards a signed subscription. Deliver a final report to the funder against the budget.

**Budget amount (naira):** 9,231,110

**Duration (number of days):** 125

**Milestone asset (pdf):** `milestones/HAQQAI_MILESTONE_3.pdf`

Total: 50,000,000 naira over 365 days.
