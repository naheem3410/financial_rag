# HaqqAI Pitch Video Script (5 minutes)


---

## 1. The Hook (0:00 to 0:30)


Imagine your bank tells you your account balance, and the balance is wrong. The computer
made it up. It sounded very sure, and it was still wrong.

This happens with AI. And in a bank, one wrong number can lose a customer, cost a big
fine, or ruin a good name.

I am Naheem Quadri. I built HaqqAI so that banks can use AI and never worry about the AI giving wrong answers.

---

## 2. Problem and Solution (0:30 to 1:30)


Banks want AI to help. To answer customers. To help staff. To read big, hard documents.
But they are afraid. Why? Because AI can be very sure, and very wrong.

So today, banks do one of two things. People check everything by hand. That is slow. Or
they use small chatbots, like Leo at UBA and ZIVA at Zenith. Those do small jobs, like
telling you your balance. Neither one can be trusted with the big questions.

HaqqAI is different. Think of a teacher and a checker. The teacher writes an answer. Then
a second checker, who never saw that answer, looks at the real papers and asks, is this
true?

If yes, the answer goes out. If no, HaqqAI says, I do not know. It never guesses.

I built this, and I tested it on real financial documents, with real AI, live. The
results were impressive. When HaqqAI gave an answer, it was right 96.6% of the time.

---

## 3. Market Opportunity (1:30 to 2:30)


Who actually needs this? Every bank.

In Nigeria there are 21 big banks. More than 860 microfinance banks. And more than 430
fintech companies. That is over 1,300 places that hold people's money, and all of them
must follow strict rules.


If each one paid what our plans cost, that is about 15 to 20 billion naira every year,
in Nigeria alone.

And it is growing. Fintechs are moving fast, so banks must move fast too. The rules about
AI are getting stricter. So banks need AI they can prove is right.

I do not see anyone in Nigeria selling that yet. HaqqAI can be the first.

## 4. Business Model (2:30 to 3:30)


How do we make money? One yearly plan, paid by the bank. Not per person. Not per
question. Simple.

Starter is 5 million naira a year. Professional is 15 million. Business is 35 million.
Enterprise starts at 75 million.

A bank starts small, with help for its customers. When it trusts us, it adds help for
staff, then for daily work, then the full safety tools. So every bank grows with us.

Is there demand? I work at a leading bank in Nigeria. I see it. Banks want tools that are
correct and can be checked. At my own bank, I already helped build a smaller version of
this. I did not guess this need. I saw this happening.
